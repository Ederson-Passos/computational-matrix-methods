\begin{abstract}

    This work investigates two computational problems involving numerical
    linear algebra and the numerical solution of a one-dimensional steady-state
    heat conduction problem. First, a symmetric matrix generated according
    to the prescribed procedure is analyzed to determine whether it is
    positive definite, followed by the computation of its Cholesky
    factorization, condition number, and the solution of the associated
    linear system. Second, the steady-state heat conduction equation in a
    unit-length bar is discretized using the finite difference formulation
    specified in the problem statement, resulting in a linear system whose
    solution provides the temperature distribution along the bar. The
    numerical results are analyzed with respect to the algebraic properties
    of the matrices, the conditioning of the linear systems, and the physical
    interpretation of the computed temperature profile. All problem-specific
    data are generated deterministically from the student's enrollment
    number according to the procedures provided in the assignment.

\end{abstract}
